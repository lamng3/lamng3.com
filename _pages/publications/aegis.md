title: "Aegis: Searching for and Defending Against Harmful Ontology Edits in Ontology-Grounded Retrieval"
description: "An ontology decides how an agent retrieves from a knowledge graph, so a few edited triples change its answers while every fact stays intact. Aegis searches for the most harmful chain of edits with a bandit at every node of the search tree, and chooses which triples to lock against the attacker's best response."
