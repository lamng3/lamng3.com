\chapter*{About these notes}

These are my notes from following \emph{Scalable Parallel Algorithms and Data Structures} (CMSC~858N, University of Maryland, Fall 2026), taught by Laxman Dhulipala, through its public schedule and readings:
\begin{center}
  \url{https://www.cs.umd.edu/~laxman/paralg-f26.html}
\end{center}
Each chapter covers one lecture or reading, in my own words: what I understood, the proofs I worked through, and the questions I still have. The mistakes are mine.

Alongside the notes, I write the algorithms myself and time them on my own machine. That code, with a harness that checks and benchmarks it, is at
\begin{center}
  \url{https://github.com/lamng3/lamng3.com/tree/main/notes-src/parallel-algorithms/practice}
\end{center}
